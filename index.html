Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers about our 5% success-based model and tender processes.

Do you charge any upfront fees or subscriptions?

No. We operate strictly on a 5% success-based fee. There are zero hidden charges, monthly subscriptions, or initial consultancy costs.

What does "success-based" mean and when is payment made?

It means we only earn when you earn. You pay our fixed 5% service fee only after you have successfully won the tender, fulfilled the order, and the government or buyer has credited the payment to your account.

How do you handle tender selection and eligibility?

We actively scan government portals matching your exact product and service categories. Before any filing begins, our team rigorously verifies your technical parameters and eligibility to ensure a high probability of winning.

What are the client's responsibilities?

Your primary responsibility is providing accurate company documents on time and successfully fulfilling the order once awarded. We handle all the heavy lifting: compliance checks, document formatting, catalogue listings, and secure bid submission.

What specific documents are required?

While requirements vary per tender, standard documents include your GST certificate, PAN, company registration (incorporation certificate), financial statements/ITR, and technical brochures. We will provide you with a customized checklist for every bid.

What happens after winning the tender?

Our job doesn't end with a win. We coordinate the Purchase Order (PO) process and guide you through the post-order supply chain, ensuring all required payment documentation is perfectly aligned for prompt government payment.

What GeM services do you offer?

Our complete end-to-end GeM (Government e-Marketplace) management includes profile creation, exact taxonomy product catalogue listing, OEM application assistance, Reverse Auction (RA) bidding, and full order processing support.
